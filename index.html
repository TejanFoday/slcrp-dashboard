✅ Positive Findings

  • CACs generally established & functioning
  • Strong community appreciation for project
  • Active participation from all demographics
  • Traditional leaders supportive
  • High interest in climate action

⚠️ Challenges Identified

  • Low mangrove survival rates in some areas
  • Illegal harvesting between districts
  • Coastal erosion in sandy areas
  • Need for alternative livelihoods
  • WASH infrastructure deficiencies
  • Engagement gaps with deselected communities

🎯 Critical Action Required

Paramount Chiefs Engagement: Urgent meetings needed with traditional leadership to address concerns from deselected communities and ensure clear communication of project selection criteria across all chiefdoms.
