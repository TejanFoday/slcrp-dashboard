# SLCRP Visual Dashboard

## Sierra Leone Coastal Resilience Project - Activities & Planning Dashboard

This interactive dashboard provides a comprehensive visual summary of the Sierra Leone Coastal Resilience Project (SLCRP) Component 3 activities and Year 2 (2026) work plan.

### 🌊 Project Overview

The SLCRP is a $26.9 million climate adaptation initiative funded primarily by the Green Climate Fund ($25M) with co-financing from Iceland's Jersey Overseas Aid Commission, Clifford Chance, and the Government of Sierra Leone.

**Target Impact:**
- 260,000 direct beneficiaries (60% women, 40% men)
- 1,000,000 indirect beneficiaries
- 75 coastal communities across 5 operational districts
- 500 hectares mangrove restoration
- 1,000 hectares mangrove conservation

### 📍 Operational Districts

1. **Kambia** - 74,802 beneficiaries
2. **Port Loko** - 70,655 beneficiaries
3. **Bonthe** - 49,157 beneficiaries
4. **Moyamba** - 52,714 beneficiaries
5. **Pujehun** - 12,672 beneficiaries

### 📊 Dashboard Features

#### Main Dashboard
- Overall project statistics and targets
- Interactive district map showing 5 coastal operational districts
- **Activity 1.2.1**: Climate Risk Assessment Training (63 participants, 31 institutions)
- **Activity 1.4.6**: Community Familiarization Tour (75 communities)
- Overall project progress summary

#### Year 2 (2026) Work Plan
- 10 planned activities totaling $202,956.30
- Organized by quarters (Q1-Q4)
- Detailed information on:
  - Activity budgets and timelines
  - Key milestones
  - Risk factors and mitigation strategies
  - Gender and inclusion focus
